MYHOME · PRIVATE HOUSEHOLD PROJECT

Vehicle data information & terms of use

MyHome is a privately operated home dashboard for its operator and authorized household members. It brings household energy, heating, rooms, cameras and vehicle readings into one interface. It is not offered as a public vehicle service.

Volvo vehicle connection

The vehicle owner authorizes MyHome through Volvo ID. MyHome requests read access to vehicle discovery, trip statistics, energy state and energy capabilities. These readings support battery, reported range, plug and charging information in the household dashboard. This Volvo integration does not request vehicle-command permissions.

Data use and storage

The dashboard uses the vehicle identifier and the readings returned by Volvo to display the authorized vehicle. Access credentials and refresh tokens are stored encrypted by the home service. The operator controls the home server and its backups. Authorization and API requests are sent to Volvo; MyHome does not sell vehicle data or use it for advertising.

The home service retains its configured credentials until the operator removes them. Volvo may expire or revoke authorization. The owner can revoke the application's access through Volvo's available account and authorization controls. The household operator is responsible for access to the home server, local data and backups.

Use within the household

Access is limited to the operator and people authorized by the operator. No payment or subscription is required by this private project. Users must only connect vehicles and home systems they are authorized to access.

Readings may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. Reported range is an estimate supplied by the vehicle service. Use the vehicle's official app and instrument panel when a decision requires current vehicle information. The dashboard does not guarantee charging availability or completion.

Contact and changes

Household users should contact their home-service operator directly for support, access changes or removal of locally stored data. The operator may update or discontinue this private project. Material changes to vehicle-data use require a corresponding update to this information and, where applicable, renewed owner authorization.
